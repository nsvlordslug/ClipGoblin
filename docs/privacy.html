Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

1. Data Collection

ClipGoblin processes videos and stores app data on your local machine. We do not collect product-usage analytics, use tracking pixels, or run behavioral telemetry.

2. Third-Party API Calls

When you use AI-powered features (caption and title generation), your clip transcript data is sent directly to the AI provider you configured (e.g., Anthropic Claude) using your own API key. ClipGoblin does not proxy, log, or intercept these requests.

3. Platform Authentication

When you connect social accounts such as Twitch, YouTube, or TikTok, ClipGoblin uses OAuth to obtain access tokens. Tokens are stored locally on your machine in an encrypted database and are used only for actions you request. OAuth authorization codes and refresh tokens pass through ClipGoblin's rate-limited authentication proxy only to exchange them with the selected platform; the proxy does not store or log those credentials. Requested actions include reading account information, fetching Twitch content, uploading videos, and, when you click Refresh stats, reading available public view and like counts for your own connected account's videos.

4. Local Storage

All app data (clips, settings, API keys, OAuth tokens, and retrieved video metrics) is stored in a local SQLite database on your computer. Data leaves your machine only when you request a feature that calls a connected platform or configured provider.

5. Twitch Data

ClipGoblin uses the connected Twitch account to list eligible VODs and retrieve the stream information needed for local clip creation. Downloaded media, chat replay data, and derived analysis stay on your machine unless you explicitly send an export to another connected platform.

6. Children's Privacy

ClipGoblin is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on our GitHub repository.
